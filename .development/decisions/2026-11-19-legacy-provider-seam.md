B044 (litellm_proxy, custom and deprecated providers) was blocked three times because four of its behaviors had no way in: the custom Python provider handlers (nothing calls PythonHooks::load_custom_providers in production), the missing custom base error, LiteLLM's global key and base chains for Aleph Alpha and litellm_proxy, and the aiohttp arm. A Deployment carries one resolved base and key and no process settings, and ChatDeployment::resolve_with fills an absent base and key from the OpenAI chain. B044 now keeps what a deployment's own params can carry, and a new deferred task, B10006, builds the startup seam (section settings, the handler map, a base-configured flag keyed by model_id) and takes the moved cases. Until B10006 lands, chat_deployments refuses an aleph_alpha, litellm_proxy or custom deployment that sets no api_key or api_base of its own (ChatSetupError::UnbuiltChain), because the OpenAI fallback would send an OpenAI key to another provider's host, or the deployment's own key to api.openai.com. B10006 lifts that refusal. Its skeleton, chat_setup/legacy_providers.rs and its mod line in chat_setup.rs, is added when B10006 comes back, since B044 edits chat_setup.rs now. B10006's startup text assumes B8613, B8610 and B9990 as planned on 11-19; re-read it before unblocking.

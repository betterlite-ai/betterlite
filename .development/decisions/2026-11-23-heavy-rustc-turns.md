In CI, every rustc build that needs over 4 GB takes turns on one lock, not just the oracle crate's two builds (#1542). On 11-23 the cache part's runner was lost three times while it built the workspace tests (runs 45129968169 and 45144639135, the second one twice). A local rebuild of the workspace crates with CI's settings (4 jobs, no incremental) peaked at 19.9 GB of summed compiler memory and spent 226 s over 14 GB: the betterlite-server tests need 10.3 GB, the betterlite-core tests 9.2 GB, and nine more builds 4.0 to 5.7 GB, while the oracle builds have shrunk to 4.8 and 4.9 GB since its helpers moved out. With those 11 builds on one lock the same rebuild peaked at 13.4 GB with no second over 14 GB, and took 1490 s instead of 1144 s. The list sits in .github/scripts/rustc-turns.sh; a crate whose test build grows past 4 GB goes on it.

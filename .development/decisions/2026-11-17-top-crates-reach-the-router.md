betterlite-python and the betterlite binary crate may depend on betterlite-router (Max). deny.toml let only betterlite-oracle, betterlite-router-fuzz and betterlite-server do so, but the Python Router tasks (B6022, B8356, B162) need router types in the betterlite-python cdylib, and B8381 implements the router's RouterSettingsTarget in crates/betterlite. Both are top crates that nothing below depends on, so the rule still keeps provider and core crates from reaching up into the router, and no loop is possible.

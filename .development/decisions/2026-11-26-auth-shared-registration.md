Auth stage tasks run side by side (Max, 11-26). Every stage task writes the same five registration files: crates/betterlite-server/src/auth.rs and, under auth/, auth_identity.rs, auth_errors.rs, failures.rs and auth_request_models/tests.rs. Each adds an AuthBackend and StageInput variant, an INSTALLED_STAGES entry and its match arms, checked on B887, B751, B879, B739, B883 and B886. Those files are in settings.overlap_allowed now, and the stage tasks list them by name instead of crates/betterlite-server/src/auth/**, so each owns only its own auth and state modules. Before this, 11 ready tasks at chain depths 17 to 47 waited on one another for auth.rs. The price is merge conflicts in those five files when two stage PRs land close together; merge-main.sh leaves them to the architect, who keeps both sides' variants and arms. B052, B053 and B054 keep auth/** until their own auth modules are known.

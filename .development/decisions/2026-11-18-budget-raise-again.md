A blocked task's line budget may be raised by rule up to three times, not once (Max). The first raise still goes to at most 1.5x the budget. The second and third go to at most 1.3x the budget they find, each to the counted lines plus 10%. Raises the architect commits count toward the three. In the night to 11-18 six tasks (B693, B604, B304, B8397, B2206, B665) stopped for the architect with budget asks the rule could not grant, each under 30% more, and each got its raise by hand. A task that asks for more than 1.3x of its raised budget, or asks a fourth time, still stops for the architect.

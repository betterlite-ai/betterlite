Two worker rungs run DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 through Vercel AI Gateway as a trial (Max, 11-19): vercel-deepinfra pins DeepInfra and vercel-pareto pins Pareto, two seats each. For the worker tokens of 11-19 00:00 to 09:36 UTC, DeepSeek direct costs 72.81 USD at a full week's share of its peak hours. Vercel adds no markup and prices the same tokens at 40.97 USD on DeepInfra (fp8, a promotional price with no end date) and 36.38 USD on Pareto (https://ai-gateway.vercel.sh/v1/models/deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017/endpoints). Each rung lists only tasks that never started, so no saved progress moves, and after 3 attempts a task falls back to the DeepSeek rungs. The env files vercel-deepinfra.env and vercel-pareto.env sit outside the repository like deepseek.env; each pins its provider through CLAUDE_CODE_EXTRA_BODY and keeps its transcripts in its own config folder. dispatch-flow.sh leaves the vercel vendors out of the check that grants a run on DeepSeek when the subscription vendors are at their limits, since a vendor that never reaches a limit would hold that grant back forever. The trial compares cache hits, decode speed, the prompt-size term and errors per request with DeepSeek direct in the same hours, and USD per task with the drop of the credit balance (GET https://ai-gateway.vercel.sh/v1/credits).

Provider and logging futures are Send, and the logging env's trait objects are Send + Sync (Max, route 1 of two). The gateway's Adapter::execute must return a Send future, and the provider calls could not, since PrePostEnv held LogSink, ApiCallLogger, InputEventSink, SentrySink and LoggerFn with no Sync bound. Twelve signatures returned LocalBoxFuture and one function carried a future_not_send expect, and B027 left the Anthropic gateway path out. The other route, a per-request LocalSet in the gateway, would have kept every provider call pinned to one thread. B9984 adds the bounds, removes the workarounds and bans LocalBoxFuture and LocalBoxStream in clippy.toml; B9986 then wires Anthropic chat into the gateway.

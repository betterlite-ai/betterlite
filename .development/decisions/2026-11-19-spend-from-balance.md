The watchdog counts the day's DeepSeek spend from the account balance instead of from the runs' usage lines (Max, 11-19). The old count summed the usage line at the end of each run's stdout, and two kinds of run never write one: worker runs cut off at the 90-minute timeout (84 on 11-19 by 10:00, about 15% of worker runs, all still working when they stopped) and reviewer runs that leave no usage. On 11-19 the old count had 76 USD by 10:00, against 96 USD in the worker session transcripts alone. From 10:05 to 11:07 the balance fell 19.87 USD while the worker transcripts summed 13.91, so reviewers and other runs are about 30% of the spend. The watchdog already read the balance every 10 minutes for the low-balance warning. It now adds each reading to logs/deepseek-balance.csv and sums the drops since local midnight. A top-up shows as a rise and adds nothing, so the spend inside the one 10-minute interval that holds a top-up goes uncounted.

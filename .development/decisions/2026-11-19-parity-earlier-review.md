The parity pass keeps its own earlier review, so a later parity round checks its own findings and reports only lines changed since, like the general reviewer has done (Max agreed to review only the changed part on 11-18). Before this, the parity pass got no earlier review and read the whole branch again every round. Of 4,506 parity reviews in the task journals on 11-19, 1,776 ran after the parity pass had already approved the branch, and 1,632 of them (92%) approved again. The shared earlier review can't be reused, since it holds the general reviewer's findings, and the parity review may report only its own rules. A round the parity pass sits out keeps its last review.
